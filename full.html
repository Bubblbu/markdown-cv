Profile Summary

Researcher and community organizer with over a decade of experience leading data-driven research and community-based initiatives, with qualitative fieldwork experience across a range of settings, and building the infrastructure that lets that work outlast any single project. Skilled at translating raw information into insight, and insight into collective action, in collaboration with communities and institutions. Brings a strong record of scholarly publication, participatory and community-centred methods, and long-term data stewardship, now dedicated to applying these skills toward housing justice and evidence-based public service.
